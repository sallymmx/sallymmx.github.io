# About Me

I am currently an Assistant Professor at the College of Computer Science & Technology, Zhejiang University of Technology. My research interests lie in Robotic Embodied Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Multi-media Technology. Specifically, my work focuses on robotic manipulation, robot perception and cognition, video action recognition, object tracking, training and inference acceleration, and video/image editing.

I received my Ph.D. (2024), M.S. (2018), and B.S. (2015) degrees in Control Science and Engineering from Zhejiang University, under the supervision of Prof. Yong Liu.

I have published over 60 papers in top-tier computer vision journals and conferences (e.g., TPAMI, TIP, TMM, CVPR, ICCV, ICLR, AAAI) and top robotics conferences (ICRA, IROS). I have also served as an Area Chair (AC) for ICCV 2025, ECCV 2026, and WACV 2026, and as a Senior Program Committee (SPC) member for AAAI 2027.

For prospective students interested in robotics and computer vision, feel free to contact me via email!
